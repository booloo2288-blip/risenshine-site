Rise'N Shine delivers a personalised package every morning, at the hour you choose: a motivational message, an image, a music recommendation and the weather where you are.

To do that, the app needs some information about you. This document explains exactly what it is, why it is needed, who receives it, and what you can ask us to do with it at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller, within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), is:

  • Gheorghe Albulescu, individual, Romania
  • Contact: risenshine.ro@gmail.com

Rise'N Shine is developed and operated by one person. The same person who writes the app answers any question about your data.

2. What we collect

2.1 Account data

  • Your email address and password, needed to sign in. The password is stored hashed and cannot be read by anyone, including us.
  • The name from your Google account, if you sign in with Google. It is used so the app can address you by name. Signing up with an email address asks for no name at all.

2.2 Preferences you tell us

  • Your preferred tone, your interests, your music genres
  • Your city, for the weather
  • Wake time, active days, time format, language
  • Gender, if you choose to provide it — used only to show you the matching exercise clips. You may leave it blank; the app works identically.

2.3 Data generated by using the app

  • Morning session data: the day, the duration, whether the voice was played, whether you completed the session
  • Content generated for you: the daily motto, weekly reflections, and the ones you choose to keep in your reflection journal
  • Technical usage events: which screens opened, whether a generation succeeded or failed, the platform and app version. They contain no name, email or IP address, but they are linked to your account identifier, so that retention and conversion can be measured across people rather than isolated sessions.

2.4 Notifications

If you allow notifications, your device receives a technical notification identifier (a "token"). It is used only to deliver your morning notification at the hour you chose.

2.5 What we do NOT collect

We do not collect health data. The app does not read sleep, steps, heart rate or anything else from Health Connect or any other health app. It does not request permission to do so.

We do not collect GPS location. For weather we use only the city name you enter.

We do not read your social media accounts and we do not retrieve any of your posts.

We never sell your data to anyone, in any form.

5. How long we keep data

  • While your account is active — we keep your data so we can deliver your morning and preserve the continuity of your profile.
  • After you delete your account — personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Technical events are detached from your account on deletion and may be kept longer, in aggregate form, for statistics.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights, and you can exercise them by emailing risenshine.ro@gmail.com:

  • Access — find out what data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct anything that is wrong
  • Erasure — ask for your account and data to be removed. You can also do this directly in the app, from Settings. Full steps: Account deletion.
  • Portability — receive your data in a format you can use elsewhere
  • Objection and restriction — object to certain processing or ask that it be limited
  • Withdrawal of consent — at any time, with no justification needed

We respond within 30 days at most. If you are not satisfied with the response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Romanian supervisory authority, ANSPDCP, at www.dataprotection.ro, or with the authority in your own country of residence.

7. Security

  • All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS)
  • Passwords are stored hashed, through an irreversible function
  • Access to data is restricted at row level, so one account cannot read another account's data
  • Access keys for external services are kept on the server and are never included in the app installed on your phone

No system is absolutely secure. If a breach occurs that puts your data at risk, we will notify you and the competent authority within the time limits set by law.

8. Minimum age

Rise'N Shine is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age. If you learn that a minor under 16 has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Automatically generated content

The motivational messages, reflections and recommendations are generated by an artificial intelligence model, based on your preferences. They are intended for daily inspiration and wellbeing. They do not constitute medical, psychological or therapeutic advice and are not a substitute for consulting a professional.

10. Changes to this policy

When the app changes, this document changes with it. The date at the top of the page always shows the last update. If an important change occurs — a new category of data, for instance — we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
