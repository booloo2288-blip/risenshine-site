Rise'N Shine

A morning that sounds like you

A personalised start to the day, delivered at the hour you choose.

What it does

Every morning, at the hour you choose, you get a notification. Behind it waits a package built that night, for that day, from what you told the app about yourself.

You search for nothing. You choose nothing. It is already there when you open your eyes.

Your morning, in four parts

A thought for the day

Written for you, in the tone you picked — calm, direct or energetic. Not a quote lifted off the internet.

An image

Matched to the mood of the morning and to the way you like to begin.

A track

From your own genres, different every day, opening straight into Spotify or YouTube Music.

Today's weather

Temperature, sky, chance of rain — for your town, not for the capital.

And a few things that don't fit in four boxes

  • VoiceYour morning read aloud while you make the coffee.
  • Short practicesBreathing, a stretch, a one-minute movement break. Skippable with no consequence.
  • Weekly reflectionOnce a week, one observation about how your mornings have been going. Keep it in the journal, or let it pass.
  • JournalWhat you choose to keep stays yours, in one place.

Who it is for

For people whose day starts in a hurry and who would like the first five minutes to be theirs. No streak counters, no scores, no guilt if one morning you don't open the app.

If a feature makes the morning feel more crowded, it does not belong here.
